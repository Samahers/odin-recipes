Watermelon and Strawberry Lemonade

Description

This strawberry and watermelon lemonade recipe blends fresh fruit into a drink! It's best served cold over ice.

Ingredients

  • 8 cups cubed seeded watermelon
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 cup fresh strawberries, halved
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • ½ cup fresh lemon juice

Steps

  1. Combine watermelon, water, strawberries, sugar, and lemon juice in a blender; blend until smooth.
